# Architecture & Product Decision Log

## ADR-001 — Position the site as a Professional AI Hub

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** `housniahmed.com` is not treated as a generic portfolio. It is a personal professional hub combining AI engineering proof, research, selected services, writing, and opportunity conversion.

**Why:** Ahmed's background spans engineering, research, entrepreneurship, digital products, automation, and AI engineering. A single chronological portfolio would undersell the coherence and evidence across these areas.

---

## ADR-002 — Use proof-first content

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** Projects, case studies, architecture, repositories, research, and verified outputs take precedence over claims and technology lists.

**Why:** The primary objective is credibility and qualified opportunities.

---

## ADR-003 — Make AHMED AI OS the signature project

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** AHMED AI OS becomes the flagship technical project of the site.

**Why:** It provides a natural place to demonstrate systems thinking, agent architecture, memory, knowledge, orchestration, evaluation, and deployment as those pieces are genuinely implemented.

**Constraint:** Planned modules must never be presented as implemented until verified.

---

## ADR-004 — English-first launch

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** Launch the public site primarily in English.

**Why:** The main opportunity target is international AI engineering, consulting, and collaboration.

French / other language versions can be added later if justified by audience data.

---

## ADR-005 — Avoid an agency-style personal site

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** The personal site will not become a replacement for separate business brands.

**Why:** `housniahmed.com` should remain the personal professional hub, while businesses such as Mediabuying.ma and products such as StoryKids retain their own identities.

---

## ADR-006 — Defer the AI portfolio assistant

**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Decision:** The AI assistant is a later feature, not a launch dependency.

**Why:** Strong source content and proof must exist before an AI interface is layered on top.
