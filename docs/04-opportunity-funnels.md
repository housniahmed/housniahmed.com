## LinkedIn strategy

Three repeatable pillars:

### Build in public
Document real engineering decisions and progress.

### AI engineering
Explain what is actually built, tested, and learned.

### AI × business
Connect engineering work to practical business problems.

Every high-value post should have a natural path back to a relevant page on the website.

## Outbound strategy

A preferred pattern is **demonstration-based outreach**:

1. Identify a real workflow or problem.
2. Build a small proof of concept when appropriate.
3. Explain the observation.
4. Link to the proof.
5. Invite a conversation.

Avoid generic claims such as "we revolutionize your business with AI."

## Job application strategy

For strategic applications:

1. Parse the role requirements.
2. Select 2–3 relevant projects.
3. Tailor the resume.
4. Link directly to relevant case studies.
5. Keep the rest of the portfolio available for context.
