# Project Vision

## Vision statement

Build a personal AI Engineering Hub that turns Ahmed El-Housni's professional experience, projects, research, and technical work into a durable system for generating qualified career, consulting, collaboration, and business opportunities.

## What this site is not

The site is not intended to be:

- a generic resume page
- a collection of every project ever completed
- a digital agency website
- an animation-heavy "AI" showcase
- a collection of unverified claims

## What it is

It is a **proof-first professional system** combining:

- personal brand
- AI engineering portfolio
- technical case studies
- research
- open-source evidence
- professional resume
- selected services
- writing
- opportunity-oriented conversion paths

## Strategic positioning

**Primary:** AI Engineer building intelligent systems, automations and digital products.

**Supporting differentiators:**

- engineering systems thinking
- applied research
- entrepreneurship and product thinking
- hands-on digital business experience
- AI engineering training and project work

## Success definition

The primary success metric is:

> **Qualified opportunities generated**

Examples:

- qualified recruiter conversations
- interviews
- AI engineering opportunities
- qualified consulting leads
- collaboration requests
- speaking / research opportunities

Traffic, impressions, and follower counts are secondary signals.

## Audience-to-path mapping

| Audience | Primary path | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter | Resume → Work → Contact | Selected projects, experience |
| Founder / business | Services → Case Study → Contact | Automation / AI work |
| CTO / technical peer | AI OS → Work → GitHub | Architecture, implementation |
| Researcher | Research → Writing | Doctoral work and publications |
| Collaborator | About → Work → Contact | Projects and current focus |

## Positioning principle

The site's narrative should connect the career arc:

```
Engineering
    ↓
Industrial / technical problem solving
    ↓
Entrepreneurship & digital products
    ↓
Automation
    ↓
AI Engineering
    ↓
AI systems
```

The narrative should explain this progression without presenting past and present roles as interchangeable.
